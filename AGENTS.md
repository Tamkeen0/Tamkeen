Product: a blind student can complete a short technical exam independently using speech or keyboard/screen reader. Professor approves a faithful accessible representation before publishing. No live unrestricted LLM in student mode. Never solve, hint, simplify away assessed reasoning, or reveal answers. Scope one circuit diagram type, English first, 3 questions, no real data. Prototype, not certified secure or accessible. No deployment or external costs without approval.
